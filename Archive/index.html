Project 2: HyperNarrative

My project is an interactive visualization of Hito Steyerl’s essay In Defense of the Poor Image. It is a non-linear representation of the various poor images Hito discusses in the article. Each page features key passages from the text and poor images that connect to the concepts. It also functions as a repository of free to access media and scholarship on the web. These artifacts are representative of the poor image ethos and are deracinated from their origins. There is a consistent theme of glitches and imperfection in my narrative’s aesthetic markers and the non-linear structure is intended to mimic falling down an internet rabbit hole.
